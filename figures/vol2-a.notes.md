# Volume 2, PDF pages 33–46 (vol2-a)

- **p.33** — Title spread of 後編. On the left a seated man writes the title characters with brushes held in his hands, mouth and feet. On the right is the preface and colophon (文化十一). No lesson.
- **p.34** — Two pages of small figures, each labelled with the kana it is built from: seated and standing men and women, travellers with hats and bundles, a figure bent forward. The kana is written beside each figure.
- **p.35** — Left: water swirls, several の written as rings and tomoe, a plum blossom drawn as a lobed ring around a 米, and crossing ruled strokes with 大. Right: dragons in cloud made of long looping の/し strokes; heads and hair tufts built on の shapes; cranes whose neck, back and legs are single written strokes (→ `tsuru-shi-he`).
- **p.36** — Left: a seated courtier drawn as a large 心 with a capped head on top, then finished with a patterned robe; a small bird. Right: Daruma sketched as a hood stroke with a し inside, then the finished Daruma on a straw mat (→ `kuge-kokoro`, `daruma-shi`).
- **p.37** — Seated nobles and a kneeling woman reading. Each is first written as a few long kana-like strokes (hooks and bowls) and then finished with hair, faces and a patterned robe.
- **p.38** — Left: Hotei holding up his sack. The sack is a large わ, and ひ, つ and ん-like strokes are written beside it (→ `hotei-wa`). Right: a standing woman sketched in a few looped strokes and then finished with a patterned kimono, with a lantern above.
- **p.39** — Left: a stretching, yawning man drawn from ハ/く/へ-like strokes, then finished. Right: an elongated-head sage sketched with a loop, ハ and と, then finished; two small turtles drawn with crossing strokes.
- **p.40** — Right: a large み and a kneeling woman seen from behind whose robe is that み. Small scarecrows made from キ/中-like strokes stand in the field (→ `onna-mi`, `kakashi-ki`). Left: a traveller under a big round hat in ruled rain, drawn first as a circle over looping strokes.
- **p.41** — Left: an elephant whose head sketch is labelled with small characters (ヌ, 九, 三, 十, 土, 七, ハ). モ, の, し and ロ are written beside it. Right: a courtesan whose hairpins are horizontal strokes and whose robe is written as イ/乃/心-like strokes, then shown finished.
- **p.42** — Left: willow branches from ノ and 八 strokes, and a hooded walker built from ノ, リ and 入 with a staff, then finished. Right: a court lady from behind with は and と written in her robe, then finished with a fan and a long fall of hair (→ `nyobo-ha-to`).
- **p.43** — Landscape written with characters. Left: 山 is the hill, 久 the pine trunk, small キ its needle tufts, 入 the roof and 回/向 the hut, followed by the finished hillside (→ `yamazato-yama`). Right: roofs from へ and ヨ, windows from 山 and 回, and a pagoda with a 士/キ finial.
- **p.44** — Left: waves written as rows of つ, and plovers written from て with a small looped head, then finished over the waves. Right: sparrows from 小 (head markings), つ/し (body) and ト/ハ (legs), then finished.
- **p.45** — Right: a woman bending to lift her skirts, written with つ, し and さ, then finished wading a stream. Left: sleeping figures wrapped in clouds, with the cloud strokes built from 山, し and く.
- **p.46** — Left: a seated man reading with a fan beside him, sketched from ヒ, ナ and a few hooked strokes, then finished on a mat. Right: pines on a headland built by stacking 三, 十, 二 and 六 (numerals written beside them), with sailboats and a fence.
