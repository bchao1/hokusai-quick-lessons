# Volume 2, PDF pages 47–60 — what each spread shows

**p.47**
- Right: a seated figure written with big kana strokes (ん, つ, ち and small ツ-like ticks) with a laughing head on top. Below it, the finished scene: two men at a lattice window, one bowing.
- Left: two き written large inside sweeping brush curves (flowing water or wind), with dragonflies drawn as crossed strokes and a band of grass ticked in at the bottom.

**p.48**
- Right: two Kannon with halos. The left one is only the long kana strokes the caption lists (人, く, ノ, し, ん). The right one is the same strokes with a face added, and she stands on a hatched lotus petal. → `kannon-moji`
- Left: three small figures built from a circle-head and kana (の, ア/カ-like hooks, 心-like dots), and 山 written flat as a mountain. Below: three finished courtiers with patterned robes.

**p.49**
- Left: large カ and 田/め-like characters beside a man carrying a standard and a laughing dancer. の, て and し are written large at lower right.
- Right: two lions (獅子) sketched in loose strokes that include a big し, then finished with curly-mane hatching and spiral-dot patterns.

**p.50**
- Left: a traveller seen from behind under an open umbrella. The sketch is a circle plus へ, ん, と and み. 石, 砺 and 居 are written beside a textured rock drawn with slanting ticks.
- Right: Kanzan and Jittoku (named in the caption), one holding a broom, with the strokes やき, へ and たせ written large and a raised scroll speckled with dots.

**p.51**
- Left: a fishmonger bent over a fish on a chopping board. The large み, は and き beside him give the back, the hooked arm and the leg. → `uoya-mi`
- Right: cormorants on rippling water. Stacked ヒ strokes and a し-plus-dash give the small birds; 水 is written in the caption.

**p.52**
- Left: a man hunched over a round board, written with や, の and う around a big rounded back.
- Right: two haloed rakan. The strokes ら, の and ん are written large, and 山 written low becomes the rock they sit on.

**p.53**
- Left: an angler on a bank with rod and line, written as ひ, る and こ. The finished figure wears a patterned coat, with a sake jar beside him. → `tsuribito-hiruko`
- Right: a woman beating cloth on a fulling block (kinuta). Her hair and robe come from a few large looping strokes written above her.

**p.54**
- Right: Daikoku carries a huge sack while standing on rice bales. The sketch writes 大, と and く: 大 is the sack, と and く are his body and robe. → `daikoku-daitoku`
- Left: a plum branch drawn with long straight strokes (ノ, ク and ル-like joints), with blossoms. A small bush warbler on the lower branch is written as へ and の (caption: うぐひすの へ の).

**p.55**
- Left: a cursive 風, whose large outer curve becomes a man bowed into the wind. Ruled diagonal wind lines, dust dots and maple leaves surround him. → `kaze-hito`
- Right: 山 written tall and stacked becomes rocky cliffs with moss ticks. A reclining man is drawn in a few kana-like strokes, repeated below as a finished figure lying on a mat.

**p.56**
- Left: Fuji written as ふじ: ふ is the notched crown and じ is the long left slope. The finished Fuji stands over dotted mist, with cedars written as 耒. → `fuji-moji`
- Right: a landscape of peaks with ぶ-like crowns, a winding river from つ/と-like strokes, and grassy banks with hatching.

**p.57**
- Left: a ghost written with う, ら and め, with long falling hair. The corner shows ヒ written over and over to make the hair strands. → `yurei-urame`
- Right: a hooded woman with a whisk, drawn from one enormous つ-like curve with こ and 九 written beside it.

**p.58**
- Left: brush exercises with no characters: wedges, triangles, zigzags, a ring and blotted shapes, each made with a flat or pressed brush.
- Right: a child peeking out of a big round bag, from か, ご and a huge し-like curve. Small gourd shapes are written as あ-like characters.

**p.59**
- Holding the brush: several hands shown gripping the brush at different angles, with the half-moon ink marks each grip makes and a brush tip shown dry and wet.

**p.60**
- Left page blank. Right: a monk dozing at a desk inside a round window. One long vertical stroke with a hook is the window edge and his robe falls in looping strokes. The caption names 丁 and の.
